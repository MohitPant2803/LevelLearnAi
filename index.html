About the Diagnostic Test

  • Dynamic Questions: Selected to test key core skills (Literacy, Numeracy, Digital, or Communication).
  • Safe Environment: Untimed, simple, single-choice queries. Designed to be taken with a mentor's guidance.
  • Dynamic Categorization: Based on the diagnostic scores, the learner is placed in an optimal level (L1, L2, L3).
  • Instant Placement: Saves directly to the dashboard, letting you map progress over time.
Mentor Tip: Read the questions out loud to the student and let them point or voice their answer.
